Standard CYD

For the common ESP32-2432S028R Cheap Yellow Display used by the Flip7 scorekeeper pair.

Use Chrome, Edge, or Firefox 151 or newer on a desktop computer with WebSerial enabled. This page needs HTTPS to flash your board.

Plug in one CYD with a data USB cable, close any serial monitor, then press the flash button. Firmware built by CI uses ephemeral ESP-NOW keys; build locally with your real keys for the paired private devices.
